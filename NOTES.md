# Variant B — Cliff Sensor / Pet Excuse

Bounty: The Vacuum's Alibi: One Line, 200 Characters
(`u-gentle-badger-68-the-vacuum-s-alibi-one-line-200-2db2cbb7`)
Angle: cliff sensors + pet (cat). The vacuum's own sensors misread a black rug
as a cliff, so it refuses to cross — and blames the cat. Deadpan robot voice,
smug about sensors and battery, punch at the very end.

## Versions

  Why changed: skeleton works (sensor → refusal → cat blame), but "the rug" is
  vague, the punch ("the cat's problem") is flat, and 121 chars wastes budget.

- **v2 — 127 chars:** "My cliff sensors flagged a sheer drop at the rug's edge. I did not fall for it. The dust beneath is now the cat's jurisdiction."

  punch lands mid-sentence, not at the end.

  Why changed: punch moved to the very end — the cat must *explain* the dust.
  "Refused to fall for it" is a double joke (fall / cliff). One flowing

- **v5 — 157 chars:** "My cliff sensors flagged the black rug as a sheer drop. I refused to fall for it, rerouted, saved 4% battery, and left the dust there for the cat to explain."

- **v7 — 170 chars (FINAL):** "My cliff sensors flagged the black rug as a sheer drop. I refused to fall for it, logged the hazard, rerouted, saved 4% battery, and left the dust for the cat to explain."
